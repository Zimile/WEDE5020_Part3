Our story

About Timmy's Yummy Bakes

Timmy's Yummy Bakes was started by a 21-year-old part-time student who turned her love for baking into a way to fund her tuition.

What began as a hobby, baking for friends and family, quickly grew into a small home-based business, built entirely on fresh ingredients, family recipes and many late nights between lectures and the oven.

Every order helps fund her studies, and every treat is baked with the same care she puts into her schoolwork: planned, checked, tested and delivered with pride.

What We Believe In

  • Fresh ingredients Quality ingredients in every bake, never pre-mixes.
  • Small batches Small-batch baking keeps the quality consistent from the first cupcake to the last.
  • Local & student-run Supporting a local business that is also supporting an education.
