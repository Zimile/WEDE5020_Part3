About Us

Timmy's Yummy Bakes was started by a 21 year old part-time student who turned her love for baking into a way to fund her tuition. What began as a hobby, baking for friends and family, quickly grew into a small home-based business, built entirely on fresh ingredients, family recipes and many late nights between lectures and the oven.

Every order helps fund her studies, and every treat is baked with the same care she puts into her schoolwork: planned, checked, tested and delivered with pride.

What we believe in

  • Fresh, quality ingredients in every bake
  • Small-batch baking for consistent quality
  • Supporting a local, student-run business
